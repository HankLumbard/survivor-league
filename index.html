How it works

Pick five castaways. Your team's score is the sum of what each of them earns as the season plays out.

Your castaway is voted off in week N N points
Your castaway reaches the Final Three 17 points
Your castaway is named Sole Survivor 20 points

The winner earns 20 points only — not 17 for the final three plus 20 for winning. Voted out week 1? That's 1 point. Week 6? 6 points. Every other pick still playing earns nothing yet, but still has a shot at the big scores.
